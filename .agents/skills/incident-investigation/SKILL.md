# Incident investigation

From the repository root, read `docs/agent-workflows/incident-investigation.md` completely and follow it. Preserve and redact evidence before changing anything, and apply no fix until a root cause is confirmed. After three falsified hypotheses, stop and post a durable handoff rather than guessing again.
